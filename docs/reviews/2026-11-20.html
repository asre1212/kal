*Cash recovery includes all proceeds and can include settlement; it does not mean a successful early limit exit. Each policy has one source with an unattainable single-leg target. Limits are the smallest modeled cent price covering the whole tranche basket cost plus net target and maker fee, never silently capped. The staged split floors the first half and assigns remainder quantity/cost/fees to the second half; an odd lot is not exactly 50/50. Cent rounding overshoots nominal targets. Costs, fees and per-leg quantities sum back to the same original entry in every policy. No oversales or sale-before-entry were found.

Within each alternative, all sources and tranches share 5% public-print liquidity and observed-book exit depth. Different alternatives each receive their own counterfactual budget; funded baseline and alternatives are not added. Fractional print credits accumulate before an integer fill, so one fill may reflect multiple prints. Tests cover shared volume, repeated prints, odd quantities, whole-basket recovery, unavailable targets, missing history and entry-time ambiguity. All 549 baseline/study backfilled fills reference archived prints (313 funded-ledger fills); timestamps are normalized before comparison. This supports ordered simulation, not actual execution or queue priority. Residual legs require the next observed book. Prints/OHLC cannot establish an executable historical market stop.
