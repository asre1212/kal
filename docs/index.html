FILL & FEE MODEL

No imaginary liquidity.

  • Immediate entries use observed asks and at most 5% of displayed top-level size.
  • Resting orders use subsequent trades at or through the limit, capped at 5% of qualifying volume. A touch is an estimate, never proof of a queue fill.
  • Buy fills must precede contingent sells. Expired orders, same-timestamp buy/sell ambiguity and candle highs/lows alone never create a fill. Exact queue priority and market impact remain unknown.
  • Quotes follow the collection cadence shown above, with a two-day assessment for an 8-hour fallback. Intervening limit fills are replayed in timestamp order; incomplete history is retried.
  • Positions are valued at available bid depth, less estimated exit fees; unpriced size is conservatively valued at zero.

THE 50/50 QUESTION

Longer expiration does not create a strangle.

YES + NO on the same contract cancel each other. For different deadlines, YES on an earlier date + NO on a later date is an outside timing-window trade.

REVIEW STANDARD

Separate signal from noise.

  • Review realized and unrealized P&L, net expectancy, drawdown, fee drag and holding time.
  • Show Wilson intervals for win rates; use an event-cluster bootstrap for return uncertainty when the sample supports it.
  • Check data gaps, market concentration, adverse selection and failed exits.
  • Keep baseline results separate from revised rules. Never erase losing trades or backfill hypothetical winners.
  • No Kelly sizing without a calibrated out-of-sample probability model; no leverage or real orders.
